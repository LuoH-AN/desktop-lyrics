# Desktop Lyrics

A minimal Android lyrics app maintained by [LuoH-AN](https://github.com/LuoH-AN).

[Download 1.0.0](https://github.com/LuoH-AN/desktop-lyrics/releases/tag/v1.0.0) · [Report an issue](https://github.com/LuoH-AN/desktop-lyrics/issues) · [简体中文](README.md)

</div>

Synchronized lyrics in a compact desktop overlay, with a native Material interface. No extra account required—use it alongside your music player.

## Get started

1. Download and install the release APK.
2. Grant Notification Access and Display over other apps permission.
3. Play music, then tap the lyric icon to the left of “⋯” in the bottom player. Further adjustments are under “⋯ → Settings”.

Requires Android 8.0+, a 64-bit ARM device, Android System WebView, and a player that exposes a standard MediaSession. Relax background battery restrictions if your device keeps closing the overlay.
